# UE6 and the Blueprint deprecation

What Epic announced about Blueprints for Unreal Engine 6, from sources read during the project's first session. Epic's own post returned HTTP 403 to the fetcher, so its wording comes from the two outlets that quote it.

## Epic's post

"The road to UE 6", https://www.unrealengine.com/news/the-road-to-ue-6, published around Unreal Fest 2026 (80 Level's report is dated 18 June 2026). Quoted by 80 Level:

- "We're moving the gameplay programming model to Verse, which transactionalizes C++"
- "UE6 will include an entirely new gameplay framework known collectively as Scene Graph, built from scratch on Verse."
- "To allow for this, Actors and Blueprints will be in early versions of UE6."
- "Eventually, these will be deprecated when the new framework is sufficiently mature, and you'll have conversion tools to move projects from one framework to the other."

## Timing

- CG Channel: "Epic said that it plans to release UE6 in early access at the end of 2027, with a stable release 12-18 months later." Epic caveated the dates with "ish".

## Reaction

- CG Channel: "the loss of Blueprints has proven to be one of the most controversial parts of this week's announcements". It notes that Blueprints let artists and designers without a programming background build content that works in game, with over a decade of learning material online.
- The Unreal Engine forum thread "They want to remove Blueprint in UE6" has at least ten pages.

## Not verified

- A Change.org petition to keep Blueprints is reported by one blog only; not checked.
- A planned visual layer for Verse is mentioned by secondary sources; no Epic wording found.
