# Audio

Source: https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/audio-in-unreal-engine?application_version=4.27

- The audio system plays in-game sounds, including Sound Cues, node-based audio assets edited in the Sound Cue Editor. [docs]
- Topics listed for 4.27 include Audio Modulation, Audio Volumes, Convolution Reverb, MIDI, soundfield ambisonics, Sound Classes, the Audio Mixer, OSC, Quartz (sample-accurate scheduling), stream caching, Submixes and Audio Synesthesia. [docs]

## For Brown Marco Creator

Phase J. The studio rule is parameterised sources built at runtime, Submixes for buses, modulation buses for volume, Quartz-style timing; Sound Cues and Sound Classes are what the studio avoids, so they go to the cut list. MetaSounds do not appear in this 4.27 index.
