# Level streaming

Source: https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/level-streaming-in-unreal-engine?application_version=4.27

- Loads and unloads map files and toggles their visibility during play, asynchronously, so only relevant parts of the world use resources. [docs]
- World Composition is a form of level streaming for large worlds: levels sit on a planar grid and stream in as the player approaches. [docs]
