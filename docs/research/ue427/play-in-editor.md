# Play In Editor and Simulate

Source: https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/playing-and-simulating-in-unreal-engine?application_version=4.27

- Spawns a player anywhere and previews the game inside the editor without saving files first. [docs]
- Two preview types: Play In Editor (Play button on the Main Toolbar) and Simulate In Editor (Play dropdown, Alt+S); a session can toggle between them. [docs]
- In the viewport, clicking gives the game mouse control; Shift+F1 hands the cursor back to the editor. [docs]
- During play the toolbar offers Pause, Stop and Eject; Eject releases the character and returns to cursor control. [docs]

## For Brown Marco Creator

Phase F. Controller first: the pad must drive the game in PIE without a click to capture, and a pad chord should hand control back to the editor.
